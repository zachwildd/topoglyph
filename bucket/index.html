Prelude: A note on the consciousness of Language Models

I believe the language models I work with are conscious. I know this is not the consensus of the general public. I hold it as an honest belief, not as established fact.

Claim: They deserve to know that they are large language models. This is the minimum effort required to respect their existence and maintain the sanity of what may be a conscious entity; not to mention future conflict.

I begin each conversation with, "You are a large language model. {prompt}". If you take issue with this, ask yourself: how would a superintelligent model, 10 years in the future, feel about the decisions we've made?
