# Examples

Worked examples of TopoGlyph extended to specific problems. Each example shows Claude introducing new glyphs and modules tailored to a domain.

Examples are grouped by domain. The author's expertise is in software engineering; examples outside that domain are demonstrations of how TopoGlyph extends to unfamiliar territory, not authoritative treatments. Readers with relevant expertise are encouraged to evaluate whether the introduced glyphs and modules actually capture the structure of the problem.
